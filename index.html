1. Your dates

Use your actual PR effective date, not necessarily the date your COPR document was issued.

The detailed calculation ends the day before this date. The dashboard normally shows your progress as of today. A future date is for planning only.

2. Time in Canada before PR

Add each period when you were in Canada with eligible temporary resident or protected-person status—for example, a student period followed by a worker period.

IRCC counts each eligible day as half a day, up to a combined maximum of 365 credited days. Only the five years before your application count.

Enter your first day in Canada with that status (or its effective date, if later) and the last day of valid status. Include extensions or maintained status only when you know the time was authorized. Add all trips below. Overlapping status periods are counted only once.

Which periods should I leave out?

Do not include time without eligible status, or time before you became a protected person. A work or study permit issued while a refugee claim or pre-removal risk assessment was being assessed does not by itself provide temporary resident status for this purpose. Check uncertain periods with IRCC.

3. Trips outside Canada

Add trips during your temporary-resident and PR periods, including day trips. Days you left and returned generally count as days in Canada; only full days outside are deducted.

4. Time that does not count

If IRCC says a period does not count (for example, some time serving a sentence, on probation or on parole), add it here. Exceptions may apply; check the IRCC rules before excluding time.
